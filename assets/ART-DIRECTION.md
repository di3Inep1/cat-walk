# 绘本美术第一批

本批使用内置 imagegen 生成，六帧猫猫与环境图集保留原始透明 PNG。森林背景转为 JPEG，以减少手机加载量。文件均保存在此目录，运行时按原始图集坐标裁切绘制。

- cat-gouache-v1.png，1536 × 1024，站立、三个奔跑姿态、上升、下落。动作脚底对齐，碰撞盒由游戏逻辑决定。
- forest-dawn-v1.jpg，1536 × 1024，第一章三关使用，慢速视差与镜像衔接。
- woodland-atlas-v1.png，1536 × 1024，草地、裂纹木桥、棚顶、树洞、路灯、鱼干。草地和木桥的可踩顶面与游戏坐标对齐。

猫猫、路灯与鱼干应用于全部章节。第一章作为绘本美术样板，码头和屋顶的背景与主要建筑仍使用原有画法。

## 实际生成提示词

### 猫猫

Use case: illustration-story. Production asset for an original hand-painted 2D side-scrolling indie game called A Cat's Ninth Walk. Make a TRANSPARENT sprite atlas, exactly 3 columns and 2 rows, aspect 3:2, preferably 1536x1024. Six equal rectangular cells, one complete character in each, no borders, captions or extra objects. Character consistent across all six: a small adorable orange tabby kitten with soft cream muzzle, chest and paws, rounded cheeks, short ears, warm brown ink outline, amber expressive eyes, fluffy curved tail, a tiny sage green neckerchief. Professional gouache and colored pencil on subtly textured shapes, softly shaded but crisp silhouette readable at 40 pixels. Mostly side-on facing RIGHT, a slight three-quarter face with both eyes visible. Same scale, body proportions, centered in each cell, feet at 82% down in every cell, generous clear padding. Top row left to right: standing relaxed idle; running with front paw extended and rear paw back; running passing pose with paws tucked under body. Bottom row left to right: running opposite contact pose; upward jumping with front paws reaching, back paws tucked and tail curved; falling with paws gently spread and ears alert. Entire kitten visible in EVERY cell. Genuine alpha transparency, no paper background, no cast floor shadows. All faces and stripes consistent, charming and well drawn, no lettering, no UI, no watermark.

### 森林背景

Use case: illustration-story. A finished hand-painted background plate for an original premium 2D side-scrolling cat adventure. Wide landscape 3:2 aspect, 1536x1024 preferred. An enchanting quiet woodland in early morning, layered old oak and birch trees with twisting organic branches, delicate leaves, fern silhouettes low at bottom, a tiny sunlit clearing in mist. Gouache washes with colored-pencil details, warm ivory sunlight and muted sage, olive and eucalyptus greens, soft honey accents. Expressive painterly brushwork and natural irregular shapes, polished storybook illustration, sophisticated restraint. This is a DISTANT BACKGROUND layer: low to medium contrast, hazy pale central 65% of the image, strongest foliage framing only far left/right/top, central action field remains spacious and bright. No traversable ground plane, no paths, no horizontal walkable edges, no platforms, no buildings, no characters, no animals, no lettering, no UI. Forest continues beyond both sides, matching gentle colors at left and right edges so it can be mirrored/repeated in game. Straight side view, no camera tilt, no perspective floor. Entire image opaque.

Exactly 3 columns and 2 rows in a 3:2 image, ideally 1536x1024, six equal 512x512 cells, generous transparent padding, no labels, no borders, no floor shadows, no text, no watermark. Top row left: a straight horizontal floating mossy earthen ledge with a CLEAR FLAT WALKABLE TOP, delicate grass and small flowers above, rich warm soil and little roots dangling below; occupies almost full cell width and about middle third of cell height. Top row middle: a straight horizontal old wooden footbridge plank, strong readable split/crack at center, knots, worn fibers, a little moss, clear flat top, occupies almost full cell width and middle third height. Top row right: a straight horizontal terracotta-and-wood orchard shed roof ledge with warm reddish shingles and wooden underside, CLEAR FLAT WALKABLE TOP, occupies full cell width and middle third height, no sloping roof. Bottom row left: a complete beautiful gnarly oak tree trunk with a small inviting arched door at the root, moss, climbing ivy and a restrained leafy crown, fully visible and centered, door threshold at lower 85% of cell. Bottom row middle: one quaint crooked wooden lantern post with a small brass framed warm yellow glass lantern, fully visible centered, no other scenery, bottom of post at lower 85% of cell. Bottom row right: one small distinct golden dried fish collectible, facing right, stitched-looking warm ochre texture, charming and crisply readable at tiny size, centered with transparent space all around. Each asset separate and wholly inside its own cell. True transparent alpha background throughout. Original high-quality painted game art, simple readable silhouettes.
